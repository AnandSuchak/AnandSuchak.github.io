LASR-MCP: Learned Adaptive Schema Resolution with Dynamic In-Tree Recovery for Enterprise Tool-Calling Agents (v2.0.3)

Anand S. Suchak • November 2026 • Preprint (Zenodo)

Enterprise Model Context Protocol (MCP) agents frequently expose large JSON Schema specifications that inflate prompt token budgets. Learned Adaptive Schema Resolution (LASR-MCP) prunes upfront schemas and dynamically injects violated constraints upon validation failure. Evaluated across N=3,840 completed trials on Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, formalizing the retry-amortization boundary in agentic tool-calling.

Performance and Tail-Latency Trade-offs Between Stateless JWT and Stateful Session Authentication Under Increasing Concurrency

Anand Suchak • 2026 • Preprint (Zenodo)

An empirically validated, full-spectrum benchmark comparing HMAC-SHA256 JWT validation against a cache-oriented stateful session architecture. Load was generated scaling from 10 to 250 HTTP/1.1 connections in Node.js, isolating the 200-connection crossover boundary and analyzing severe p99 tail-latency degradation under sustained concurrency.
